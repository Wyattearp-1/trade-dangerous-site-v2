    How it works

    This is a static site. The route search runs entirely in your browser (in a background thread) against a market snapshot that a GitHub Actions workflow rebuilds from the public Spansh station dump.

    Goal: most credits. Every hop is scored by the real cargo profit it earns, and the search keeps the richest partial routes. Distance is only a reach limit; it is shown for information and never traded off against profit.

    Every station with a market is included and searchable. Prices far outside a commodity’s normal range are discarded as bad data, and routes ignore prices older than your “max price age” setting. Always sanity-check a route against live prices (every station links to Inara) before you commit your cargo.
